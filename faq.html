What services are represented in this portfolio?

The fictional AUREVIA concept covers residential and commercial interiors, luxury homes, architectural planning, space planning, furniture and material selection, 3D visualization, and consultation. No real design service is being offered through this demo.

How long would a project take?

A real schedule depends on the brief, approvals and delivery scope. This fictional portfolio cannot promise a project timeline; any example timings elsewhere on the site are illustrative only.

How does the consultation work?

The consultation page is a demonstration of a possible enquiry journey. AUREVIA is not a real studio, so no consultation can be booked here.

How is a project budget considered?

The demo form includes budget ranges to show how an enquiry might be structured. They are not a price list, quote, or minimum project value.

What happens when I submit the contact form?

The form validates entries in your browser only. It does not send or store your name, email, phone number, or project details.

How many design revisions are included?

There is no live contract or revision policy for this fictional demo. A real project would define its review stages and scope in a written agreement.
