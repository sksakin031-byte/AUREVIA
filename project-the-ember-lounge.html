01 · The concept

A space shaped
by its brief.

An intimate sequence of seating moments within a taller hospitality room.

A warm, low-lit lounge concept for a fictional boutique hotel. The purpose of this study is to show how a design idea can respond to the needs of a fictional place without losing its atmosphere.

02 · Challenge & response

The constraint
became the cue.

A clear spatial question creates a more useful starting point than a decorative solution.

The challenge

The tall room felt too open for relaxed conversation and informal gathering.

The response

Curved banquettes, a suspended brass ceiling detail and pools of soft light bring a more human scale to the room.

03 · Design objectives

A few clear
priorities.

These fictional objectives guide the concept from the first plan to the finishing palette.

  1. —Create smaller social settings
  2. —Give the tall room a warmer scale
  3. —Balance low lighting with comfortable visibility

04 · Space planning

Let the plan
do the quiet work.

Curved banquettes divide the tall room into intimate seating pockets while leaving a clear path through the lounge.

05 · Materials & finishes

Tactile, calm
and connected.

The palette is presented as a concept direction, not a specification for construction.

  • Rust velvet
  • Walnut
  • Brushed brass
  • Textured plaster
